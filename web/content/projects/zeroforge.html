The Plain Facts, Carved

What It Is

ᚏ
Zeroforge — Patient Zero, forged from the depths of Athlone — the origin-point artifact of the garden: a forge of agents and experiments from which the later working tools of the household were themselves annealed. It resists tidy description because it is exactly the thing that came before the tidy versions: rough tools, first cuts, proofs that the fire was hot enough to work with. It is held as it was made, unpolished, because the unpolished state is the point — everything since is downstream of what cooled on this anvil. It exists because a forge has to have a zeroth casting — and Athlone, in the middle of Ireland, is where this one came up out of the ground.
ᚏ

Told in the Seanchaí Tradition

The Build Story

ᚏ

The Tuatha Dé Danann burned their ships on the landing — that is the exact word of the tradition — so that nothing would be simple to return to: the sea-glittering hulls put to the torch on the strand, and the people turned inland to the battle with no way home but forward. The Norse echo it further north: boats burned behind the war-band, the same covenant by fire. This garden's own zero was gentler but of the same shape — a forging in the depth of Athlone, in the middle of Ireland where a ford and a crossing have made everything since possible, and Patient Zero cooling on the anvil with no intent of being prettied up. What came off that anvil went on to sire the cleaner tools; the first spore, carried up from the depths, never pretended to be other than first. It stands in the quiet corner of Grove VII with the ships' ash still on it, and the moss — which knows a foundation when it grows on one — has ringed it round like a votive.
