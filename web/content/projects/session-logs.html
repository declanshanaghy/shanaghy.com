The Plain Facts, Carved

What It Is

ᚔ
A repository of session logs and artifacts — the working ephemera of coding sessions: transcripts, outputs, half-thought notes, and the small relics that fall out of an agent's hands while it works. It is held as plain HTML and text, no build, no framework — files that open as themselves. The logs are kept as they were made, so a session can be read back later the way it actually happened. It exists because the interesting part of the work is often the middle: the wrong turns, the recovered errors, the moment the fix lands. Those do not survive in a changelog, so they are shelved here instead, in their original ink.
ᚏ

Told in the Seanchaí Tradition

The Build Story

ᚏ

The monastic scribes of the west copied their vellum not because the words were going to change but precisely so they would not — the copying was the keeping, and the keeping was the covenant. A page left in the weather for one winter is a page lost; a page copied by candlelight, badly, in a cold stone cell, is a page that crosses centuries. So runs this shelf: every session that mattered, its transcript and its artifacts rolled and laid down while the ink is still warm, with no thought of tidying what the session actually said. Some pages are heroic and some are foolish, and the scribe does not judge — he files both under the same small flame. The moss has learned its way around the shelf by now, and keeps a respectful silence.
