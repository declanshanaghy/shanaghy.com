Front

Gemini prompt

Edit the first image, the front view of a white clay memorial sculpture. Keep the camera angle, framing and composition: the baby angel Josephine in a ribbed knitted bobble hat and buttoned knitted cardigan (as in photos 6 and 7), eyes peacefully closed, holding a blank scroll, with large lightning-veined wings; her father kneeling on the left and her mother kneeling on the right, looking at each other; a blank tablet behind each parent; a blank ribbon banner across the front; a low rugged base of stacked angular faceted stones. The man is a sculpted likeness of the man in photos 2 and 3 (broad face, strong jaw, short slightly tousled hair swept up and back, clean-shaven, no glasses); the woman of the woman in photos 4 and 5 (long straight dark hair past her shoulders, a hairband, her face shape, eyes and nose, no glasses). Both calm and solemn with mouths gently closed. Josephine's lower body is a clearly sculpted baby, never a shapeless mass: she sits upright on the top stone, below her cardigan she wears a white disposable diaper (as in photo 7), and her chubby bare baby legs (as chubby as the legs in photo 8) hang down over the front edge of the stone, knees bent, with small bare feet resting flat on the stone below; legs and feet are joined to the stone with no gaps. Seen from the front, both legs are fully visible side by side, from the diaper down to the toes, two matching legs of equal length with two matching feet: the scroll is held up against her chest with its lower end above her knees, and nothing (scroll, parents, tablets, hair) passes in front of her legs. Nothing else is added anywhere: no extra hands, arms or figures, no planks, sticks, posts or props, no extra hats or hoods. Each hand has exactly four fingers and a thumb. Design it to 3D-print with as little support as possible: the kneeling man's hand rests flat and fully in contact on top of his raised knee, palm and all four fingers pressed onto the knee with no gap underneath; his other hand lies flat against his thigh; both parents' arms are close against their bodies with no gaps; the scroll is held tight against the baby's body; the ribbon banner lies on and is joined to the stone base along its length, no free-hanging ends; no thin protruding parts, no floating pieces, no deep undercuts; overhangs are gentle slopes. All-white unpainted clay, plain light grey seamless background, soft even studio light. No color, no text, no symbols.
