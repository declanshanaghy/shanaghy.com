The Plain Facts, Carved

What It Is

ᚔ
An early experiment in deploying to Vercel — a small JavaScript project pushed to the platform to learn, end to end, what a modern deployment pipeline actually does. The point was the plumbing rather than the payload: the push, the build, the preview URL, the production domain — the whole road from a local folder to a page an stranger could load. It exists because at some point the only way to know what a platform answers is to push something out and listen. The answer came back in 2022: it renders.

Told in the Seanchaí Tradition

The Build Story

ᚏ

The first coracle crossing left the monks guessing which shore would answer — they set out from the west of Ireland with a hide boat, a prayer, and no return address, and whatever answered out of the fog was going to be the truth of it. The garden's own first crossing was of the same shape, if a gentler sea: one small vessel of JavaScript, pushed out onto the current of a deployment platform with no certain knowledge of the far shore. The build ran like a tide under the boat; the fog on either side was called preview and production, and neither promised anything. Then the answer came across the water, plainly, in the old tongue of the web: it renders. There have been quieter landings since, and larger; but the first shore is the one the saga remembers, and its candle still burns at the top of the stone.
