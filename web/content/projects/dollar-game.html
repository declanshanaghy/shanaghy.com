The Plain Facts, Carved

What It Is

ᚔ
A TypeScript game about grinding out dollars — coin by coin, dollar by dollar, a small idle-and-incremental-style toy about the patient accumulation of a fortune. It is built in TypeScript, with the game state typed and the grind loop running in the browser: numbers go up because they are asked to, and the asking is the game. Turn-based, tabletop-spirited, quiet. It exists because every tabletop keeps its own herds to tally, and this one wanted a digital field where the counting itself was the play.
ᚏ

Told in the Seanchaí Tradition

The Build Story

ᚏ

The Táin Bó Cúailnge was fought over counted cattle — two bullfights of the soul, whole armies moved because one side had done its arithmetic and the other had done it differently. The war of the age since has mostly been quieter and the herds smaller: the dollars, ground out coin by coin on a tabletop nobody bleeds for. This garden kept its own count of it — a small typed field where the grind is the war and the ledger rises like the dust of the route. The reels of the turn-based tabletop screen played beside this stone long into that winter, artillery of the small denominations trading shots across the felt, until the count closed. The old epic ends with the bulls dying of each other and the herd dispersed; this one banks the coin and asks the player back for tomorrow, which is, when you lay it against the Cúailnge, the more merciful arithmetic.
