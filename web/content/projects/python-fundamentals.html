The Plain Facts, Carved

What It Is

ᚔ
Notes and exercises on Python fundamentals — the working-through of the language from its roots: types, structures, functions, the standard library, and the idioms that make Python sound like Python. It mixes written notes with runnable exercises: each concept captured in prose and then immediately proved by hand in code, so the understanding is earned rather than read. The repository's exercises are worked as TypeScript-era tooling looks on, which is to say: whatever the file says, the point is what running it teaches. It exists as an act of learning in public — the fundamentals walked again, slowly, on purpose, with every step written down.

Told in the Seanchaí Tradition

The Build Story

ᚏ

Fionn mac Cumhaill cooked the Salmon of Knowledge for the poet Finegas, and in the turning of it burned his thumb on the fat — and when he put the thumb to his mouth, the wisdom of the thing came in with the scald. That is the whole method, and there is no other: the knowledge lives in the burn. So this shelf of exercises was built the same way — every fundamental of the Python tongue taken up by hand and tested against the fire of actually running, each little error a small scald taken on purpose, each note written down while the thumb still stung. The old texts say Fionn could answer any question by putting his thumb behind his teeth; the modern form is quieter — an exercise file reopened at midnight, and the answer arriving in the shape of a traceback resolved. The garden keeps the shelf mossed and green, because that is what knowledge prefers to grow on.
