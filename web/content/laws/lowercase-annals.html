Commit in the lowercase tongue — type: subject.

The Mossy Dekalogue · Law 6 of 14Commit in the lowercase tongue — type: subject. For the log is the garden's annals, and annals must scan.

Every commit is a sentence in a book that machines read first. Fix the grammar and the book indexes itself: the type is the verb, the subject the clause, and `git log` becomes a query language — search by intent, bisect by meaning, generate the changelog from the annals that already scanned. The lowercase tongue is not affectation; it is signal-to-noise made policy.

Annals that scan predict; annals that shout confuse. Write for the machine that greps and the human who reads.
