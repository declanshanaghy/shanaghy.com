Law 5 · Handcrafted Over Platform

Handcrafted over platform.

The Mossy Decalogue · Law 5 of 10Handcrafted over platform. No CMS stands between the teller and the told; the repo is scripture, the push is the mass.

The question is never 'what can run it' but 'what must'. A static garden needs a static host: bytes in, bytes out, TLS at the edge. Every layer beyond that — clusters, pods, orchestrators — is a standing army hired to guard a rock. The decree holds the line: handcrafted over platform, and what must be dynamic grows on cork, reached by tunnel, never rented by the hour.

The platform you don't run cannot fall on you at three in the morning. Keep only what you can carry.
