Handcrafted over platform.

The Mossy Dekalogue · Law 6 of 14Handcrafted over platform. No CMS stands between the teller and the told; the repo is scripture, the push is the mass.

The question is never 'what can run it' but 'what must'. A static garden needs only bytes in, bytes out, TLS at the edge — the ground beneath the bytes is a choice, not a creed. The garden tried the cluster's Autopilot ground for three days, two beds from one push, and folded the second bed back into the earth; the refusal holds and Pages keeps the garden alone. The handcraft never changed, and what must be dynamic still grows on cork, reached by tunnel.

The platform you don't run cannot fall on you at three in the morning. Keep only what you can carry.
