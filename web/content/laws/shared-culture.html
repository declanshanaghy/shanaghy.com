Law 2 · Shared Culture, Personal Memory

The culture is shared; the memories stay personal.

The Mossy Decalogue · Law 2 of 10The culture is shared; the memories stay personal. Two mosses never grew from one spore-swap.

Culture is the replicable part — the decrees, the tongue, the rites, the naming — and it travels freely: same spores, every stone. Memory is the stateful part, and it stays where it grew, because state that crosses machines is contamination wearing a gift's face. This is immutable-infrastructure truth told in moss: ship the culture, never the state.

Share what you believe; keep where you grew. The day the memories sync is the day the fleet has one mind to lose.
