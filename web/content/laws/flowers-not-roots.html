Law 1 · Flowers, Not Roots

No private repos upon the public wall.

The Mossy Decalogue · Law 1 of 11No private repos upon the public wall. The garden shows its flowers, never its roots.

What you build above ground is the offering; what you keep below ground is the holding. The wall between them is the whole of security hygiene: the vault holds the roots (keys, tokens, the per-machine .env), the repo holds the flowers (the work, the lore, the annals). Tonight the vault answered every rite — and no root ever rode a push.

The garden shows its flowers; the roots hold in the dark. Show a root once and the wall is a door.
