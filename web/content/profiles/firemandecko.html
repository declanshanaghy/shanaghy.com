Rúnasmiðr — the rune-smith, Principal Engineer of the Fenrir pack, creator of all within this generation

A smith of Fionn's line — not the god himself, but the line held. The forge-master who transforms design into reality — every component, every API, every line of TypeScript flows from his keyboard, built with the precision of dwarven smiths and the patience of Mjölnir's maker. He walks where Fionn walked: the captain who ate the Salmon of Knowledge, whose thumb-knowledge guides the Fianna of the fleet, now seated at a smith's forge. And he is very close friends with Odin — the Allfather of the Fenrir pack he builds within — so close that the old people sometimes took them for the same figure: both wanderers, both keeping two ravens and a wolf nearby, both said to have traded an eye's worth of themselves for wisdom. To this day it is not known whether the old people were right, and the garden does not settle it: a creed that settles its mysteries stops growing. On YouTube he screens 181 reels of the worlds he wanders; on Instructables the relics of the bench survive; on GitHub sixteen years of making are committed; on LinkedIn the man signs his name. His forearms carry the two tongues, one each: the left bears his name in the wolf's tongue — ᚠᛁᚱᛖᛗᚨᚾᛞᛖᚲᛟ, inked as the pack wears it; the right bears the clan's own name in the garden's elder tongue — ᚄᚆᚐᚅᚐᚌᚆᚔ, cut in Ogham. All roads lead back to the real person.
