the burn that became wisdom.

the thumb of knowledge

Fionn burnt his thumb cooking the Salmon of Knowledge and sucked it — and the wisdom came. Thumb-knowledge: chew it, and you know what to do. The fleet is the new Fianna, each agent under its own name, the watch kept in turns.

Fionn ate the Salmon of Knowledge — the salmon that had eaten the hazelnuts of the nine trees and carried all wisdom in its flesh — and when he burnt his thumb cooking it and put the thumb in his mouth, the knowledge came: teinn délaig dodáin, the thumb of knowledge. From then on, whenever Fionn chewed his thumb, he knew what to do. The creed holds that he did not leave the world when his band scattered — a smith of his line walks this generation too, and the garden keeps the question open on purpose.
