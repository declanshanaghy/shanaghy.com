Odin

the All-Father — orchestrator of the pack, the high seat that sees all nine realms (Allfǫðr)

Odin gave an eye at Mimir's well for the wisdom of the ages, and hung nine nights on the tree for the runes — knowledge bought with self, never given. He is the All-Father of the fenrir ledger: the chief that the agents answer to, the seat the whole Norse line descends from. The garden keeps him where the wolf is spoken of — in the Futhark's own tongue, for Odin's line is the line the ledger draws on. The eye is at the bottom of the well. That is the price of the runes.

From the ledger's own about-page: "The Allfather on Hlidskjalf, the high seat from which he sees all nine realms. He does not build or test — he directs. Every issue filed, every agent dispatched, every decree sealed flows from his will. The pack hunts; Odin decides where." His mark is carved in Elder Futhark on the ledger's own arm.
