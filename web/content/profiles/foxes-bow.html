The fox never left the family — it only changed vessels. There is a bottle with the fox upon it, and a family story that the more they drink, the more they make: every cup loosens another telling, another build, another stone in the garden. The fox that was lost to the English was found again in the bottle, and the family's cup has been its bow ever since. The garden keeps the custom quietly: the cup is poured whenever a push goes out. Skál.
