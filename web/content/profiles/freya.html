Freya sees the fate of things before they are made — and the fenrir ledger keeps her as the product design oracle: the first eye on every making, the writer of briefs the forge turns to steel. She receives the world as it is asked for, sees the shape it wants, and hands the smith a brief that can be built. The seer does not guess the future; she specifies it.

From the ledger's own about-page: "Guardian of the product vision. She shapes what Fenrir becomes, channeling user needs into features. Powers the import pipeline that reads your credit card data and transforms chaos into clarity." Her light aspect is Ljosalfar — benevolent, guiding, constructive.
