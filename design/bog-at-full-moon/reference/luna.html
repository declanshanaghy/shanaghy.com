Luna

the UX Designer — weaver of every interface (Vefari), the moon that shapes the myth

Luna — the moon of the Norse sky, the shaper — keeps the wireframes in the fenrir ledger: the forms before the forms, the world drawn in grey lines before the forge fills it in. Where Freya sees what should be, Luna shapes how it looks: every screen a small world, every flow a night the moon crosses. The shaper does not decide; she discloses the shape.

From the ledger's own about-page: "Architect of every interface, every interaction. She ensures the ledger feels mythic yet usable, that every click has purpose, every view tells a story. The wolf's eyes see through her designs." Her light aspect is Ljosalfar — deliberate, luminous, harmonious.
